# SOP — Performance

## Purpose
Frame budget, draw calls, DPR scaling, shadow settings, LOD, loading and warm-up.

## Inputs
Renderer info, frame timings.

## Outputs
Quality presets Low/High/Auto.

## Constraints
Under 100 draw calls; ≤3 lights; one 1024–2048 shadow map; DPR ≤2 (1.5 mobile); fixed 120 Hz sim with render interpolation and max-steps clamp; compileAsync before reveal.

## Tests (must pass before merge)
npm run verify: headless Playwright race averages ≥ 58 fps on Low with 8 karts; bundle JS ≤ 1.5 MB gzipped; first playable ≤ 6 s on a throttled connection.

## References
utsubo 100 tips; Fix Your Timestep.

## Decisions
_(append dated one-liners as they are made)_
- 2026-09-23: Quality Auto is a pure governor (src/performance/governor.ts): 1 s windows after a 2 s warm-up, hitches over 250 ms ignored. Below 55 fps it steps resolution down 10 % to a pixel ratio of 1, then turns shadows and post off (Low), then steps resolution to half. Never steps up mid-race; a race that held 59+ fps earns one step back at the next start.
- 2026-09-23: Pixel ratio cap 2 on desktop, 1.5 on touch screens (pointer: coarse).
- 2026-09-23: Measured on an M4 Pro at 2048×1536: sim 0.06 ms per tick, render and post 3.5 ms per frame, about 60 draw calls, 217 k triangles. JS bundle 241 KB gzipped (`npm run check:bundle`, limit 1.5 MB). The headless Playwright fps run is still not built.
- 2026-09-23: Dev-only soak tools in main.ts: `kart.step(n, ms)` runs the real loop by hand (works with the tab hidden) and `kart.autopilot(true)` lets the AI drive the player (AiDriver.drivePlayer). A full Summit Cup Grand Prix ran through menus, HUD, audio and effects with zero errors, at about 15× real time.
- 2026-09-23: With AI models everywhere, triangles (not fill) became the cost: up to 1.6 M a frame. Fixes: far and sky props cast no shadows (an InstancedMesh is never culled per instance, so every copy drew into the shadow map); the most repeated props simplified to about 1,200 to 1,500 triangles and the racers to about 10 k (gltf-transform simplify); the small windmills got their own simplified model. Now 540 k to 870 k triangles and 61 to 73 draw calls a frame on every track, 5 to 7 ms on an M4 Pro at DPR 2.

- 2026-09-24: Re-measured after the land, tunnel and AI work (M4 Pro, 1274×1726 at pixel ratio 2, 8 karts on autopilot): sim 0.035–0.09 ms a tick (0.07–0.18 ms a frame), render CPU 0.46–0.72 ms, render with the GPU flushed 5.3–6.2 ms; 51–74 draw calls; 0.5–0.6 M triangles in the scene. No change needed; Auto lowers resolution on slower machines.
- 2026-09-24: Auto on a capped display: on a 30 fps battery saver or a 50 Hz screen the governor went to half resolution with no shadows or post and never came back, and the frame rate never moved. Now a descent remembers where it began; if the floor is less than 5 % (`capGain`) faster, the rate is the display's limit, not load: full quality comes back once (about 12 s in, usually on the title screen) and that rate becomes the `ceiling`, held at 90 % of it (`capShare`). Load under that still steps down, and a frame rate 10 % over the ceiling clears it. Not told apart by steady frame times alone: a vsync-locked 30 fps on a slow GPU is just as steady.
- 2026-09-24: The pixel ratio follows the screen: resize and a `(resolution: Ndppx)` media query (watchPixelRatio, re-armed on each change; a move between screens need not fire resize) both call applyRender, which re-reads the cap and rebases the governor. Before, a move from a Retina screen to a 1x one kept rendering at 2 (four times the pixels), and the reverse stayed blurry.
- 2026-09-24: Detail review, shadow acne (game/shadow.ts SUN_SHADOW): the sun's one 2048 map over a 120 m box now has normalBias 0.06 m (about one 6 cm texel) and bias -0.0001 (4 cm at far 400 m). With no bias the low sun striped the mine portal, the tunnel walls, the cliffs and the mesas; kart contact shadows still touch the wheels. No cost.
- 2026-09-24: `npm run verify` grew two stages: the bundle gate (vite build, then scripts/check-bundle.mjs: 327 KB gzipped, limit 1.5 MB) and a headless frame budget (src/performance/frameBudget.test.ts: every track with 8 karts and the effects layer; visible draws plus shadow casters ≤ 80, leaving 20 for the post chain and live effects; scene triangles ≤ 400 k). Measured: 35–45 draws plus 15–21 shadow casters, 133–269 k triangles. Not in verify, by choice: a headless fps run (a CI runner has no GPU, so its frame rate says nothing about a player's laptop) and Lighthouse (needs a browser download); fps is measured by hand in a muted page (kart.stats()), a11y by the jsdom checks in ui-hud test 16.
- 2026-09-24: Mid-laptop check (M4 Pro, 1920x1080 window, Harbor Loop mid race, 8 karts; each frame timed through a 1-pixel readPixels so the GPU work is inside the number; gl.finish does not wait in Chrome). Whole frame: High at DPR 2 (3840x2160) 9.3 ms, DPR 1.25 4.4 ms, DPR 1 3.6 ms; Low at DPR 1 1.4 ms, at 0.5 1.1 ms; sim alone about 0.1 ms. At DPR 1 the post chain cost 1.5 ms and the shadow map 0.2 ms, and most of the post cost was 4x MSAA on the half-float buffer (DPR 2: 9.0 ms with MSAA 4, 6.9 with 2, 5.2 with none). So a Retina screen (pixel-ratio cap 1.5 or more) now draws no MSAA (post.ts `msaaSamples`, keyed to the screen's cap, not the governed ratio, so a step down never turns MSAA back on). Estimate for a mid laptop (integrated GPU about 4 to 5 times slower than an M4 Pro's): a 1080p screen at DPR 1 about 15 to 18 ms on High, so the governor keeps 60 with a small resolution step or Low (6 to 7 ms); a Retina MacBook Air about 5.2 ms × 3.5 = 18 ms at full resolution, one or two resolution steps to 60. This is an estimate from the ratio, not a run on the machine.
- 2026-09-24: Hitch hunt (muted headless Chrome on an M4 Pro, 1920x1080, full 3-lap races on autopilot; every frame's time, its WebGL compile, link and upload calls and long-animation-frame entries logged, plus CDP traces with thread CPU time and heap samples). The stalls were shaders compiled the first time a material was drawn, 120 to 660 ms each on Chrome's Metal backend: 11 to 17 at once on the countdown's first frame (117 to 600 ms), then one whenever a prop, creature, item, model or shadow variant first came into view, and 3 more at the Final Lap Shift (it rebuilds the balloons, coins, pads and ramps with new materials, and three had freed the old shaders with the old materials). Also: each sky painting (2688x1152) decoded and uploaded on its first frame (25 to 28 ms), a model texture on first sight (up to 190 ms), and the governor itself: compile stalls pulled its 1 s windows under 55 fps, so it stepped down on the title screen (Canyon started at 0.9 Low, the 2560x1440 run at 1.4) and at 4x CPU went Low mid-race, recompiling every shader at once (a 633 ms frame). Worse with vsync off (a GPU kept busy compiles slower). Not causes: GC (scavenges 0.3 to 0.6 ms, about one every 1.5 s), HUD style, layout and paint (about 0.2 ms a frame).
- 2026-09-24: Race-start warm-up (src/performance/warmup.ts). load() compiles every material in the scene, hidden ones too, through renderer.compile into a stand-in render target (a shader's key only asks target or screen), off the main thread where the browser has KHR_parallel_shader_compile; the loop then holds (no tick, no draw, the last frame stays up) until every program reports ready and the start and shift sky paintings are in (at most 3 s), and draws everything once (hidden objects shown, culling off, every empty instance pool one instance long) so the shadow pass's shaders, the GPU's pipeline states and every texture upload happen there. The countdown's first tick comes after it. Measured: load task 100 to 140 ms, hold 0 to 430 ms (0 when the title race already compiled the shaders), the draw of everything 8 to 10 ms.
- 2026-09-24: Compiled shaders are kept for the page's life (one extra use each, at most 256) and each custom shader keeps a never-drawn copy of its material (three forgets a ShaderMaterial's source with its last material); the old race is freed after the new one's shaders compile. The Final Lap Shift's rebuilt materials and the next race reuse them. warmup.test.ts races Boardwalk, Canyon and Harbor to the flag (items, creatures and the shift included) and fails on any shader key not in the scene at load.
- 2026-09-24: Instanced shadow casters share a depth material of their own (src/performance/shadowDepth.ts): the one shared depth material flipped between instanced and plain casters every few draws, and three re-derived its shader on every flip (13 % of everything the game allocated).
- 2026-09-24: Governor: a frame over 4 times the last window's average is a stall, not judged (unless more than 3 in a row); one short window only arms it and a second confirms, the slower of the two being the rate to beat (a burst of effects, or a window half in a heavier scene, no longer steps down or fools the cap check); resolution falls by the square root of fps over goal in one change (3 changes where it took 6); a race-start step up that fails is never tried again (no flip-flop race to race); Low and back compile their shaders in the background while the old quality still draws; every change waits for a calm moment (no drift, boost, spin or jump, at most 3 s); Low picked in Settings holds like a race start. Checked live: 4x CPU and 2560x1440 at 2 never step now; 7680x4320 steps once (2 to 1.8) just after the go and holds; 8x CPU goes Low once (a 67 ms switch frame at 8x) and one resolution step 2 minutes later; none oscillate.
- 2026-09-24: The canvas has MSAA only when the post chain would (msaaSamples: none on a pixel-ratio cap of 1.5 or more). At 5120x2880 its 4x buffer made each governor resolution step a 50 to 150 ms reallocation (now 8 to 31 ms) and cost a resolve every frame; 2560x1440 at 2 went from 4 frames over 20 ms to none. On a sharp screen only Low (drawn straight to the canvas) and the racer-screen turntable lose MSAA.
- 2026-09-24: The render side of a frame (session.frame and vfx.frame) allocates about 10 KB and keeps none (allocation.test.ts holds it under 24 KB and 64 B); the session and the item view no longer build arrays and objects every frame. The 120 Hz sim allocates about 80 KB a tick (AI line reading, kart stepping, deterministic math): not a hitch today, a follow-up for those systems.
- 2026-09-24: Results, full 3-lap races, muted headless Chrome, M4 Pro, 1920x1080 at 1 unless noted, frames from the countdown's first tick to the flag, before → after. Still to do: the Final Lap Shift rebuilds the track's chunks and dressing on the spot (Canyon 8 to 14 ms of script on the M4 Pro, 75 ms at 4x CPU: its one remaining hitch on a slow machine); build the shifted geometry during the warm-up instead (track-builder).

## Lessons (repair loop writes here)
_(error → cause → fix → rule; newest first)_
- 2026-09-24: **Every race start on a 4G line sat out the warm-up's 3 s cap, and the title showed over a blank canvas for 4 s or more.** Cause: 13 MB of racer and scenery models were asked for at once as the script started; on HTTP/2 the 40 downloads shared the line with the few small files the title and the race wait on (on Slow 4G the title's font took 3 s, the sky paintings had not come 13 s in). Fix: performance/loadQueue.ts, 3 background files at a time, the most wanted first, after the title's own. Rule: nothing big is fetched all at once; background files go through the queue, and a screen's own files come first.
- 2026-09-24: **WebGL drawn in software (Chrome's SwiftShader, as on a blocklisted GPU or a VM) ran 350 ms frames at 1600x900 and kept full quality: 2.8 fps for good.** Cause: the governor skipped every frame over the 250 ms hitch limit, and here every frame was over it, so no window was ever judged. Fix: long frames that keep coming count after three in a row, like the ratio hitches; a lone one still is not (governor.test.ts). The same run now goes Low at the floor within seconds. Rule: a filter that drops outliers must not drop the steady state: test the governor at 3 fps as well as at 30 and 60.
- 2026-09-24: **Mid-race frames of 120 to 660 ms, more with vsync off.** Cause: three compiles a shader the first time its material is drawn; a race met new ones to the flag (props and creatures coming into view, shadow variants, items, the shift's rebuilt dressing), and freeing a race's materials freed their shaders, so every race compiled them again. Fix: warmup.ts compiles everything at load, holds the countdown until done, draws everything once, and keeps compiled shaders. Rule: nothing may bring a new shader key mid-race; build and compile it at load (warmup.test.ts fails on one).
- 2026-09-23: **`renderer.compileAsync` threw an uncaught "reading 'isReady'" when a track loaded.** Cause: its readiness poll runs in a callback and reads a program that a disposed session's material no longer has, so `.catch` cannot see it. Fix: removed it; the first frames compile as before. Rule: do not start async GPU work on objects a scene swap may dispose; if warm-up is needed, compile synchronously inside the loading step.
