# SOP — VFX and juice

## Purpose
Drift sparks tiers, boost flames and FOV kick, chromatic aberration, speed lines, screen shake, tyre marks, dust, hit reactions, pickup pop, position flourish, lap banner, confetti, idle life.

## Inputs
Race events.

## Outputs
Particles, post-FX parameters, camera modifiers.

## Constraints
Trauma-based shake with rotation; hit-stop 60–90 ms; all effects gated by reduced-motion; post-FX in one EffectPass; N8AO High only.

## Tests (must pass before merge)
Frame time under 12 ms on Low with all effects firing at once in a stress scene; the 12-item checklist in research plan §7.2 all ticked.

## References
Juice It or Lose It; Art of Screenshake; pmndrs postprocessing.

## Approach
_Written 23 Sept 2026 from research plan §4.7 and §7.2 (the 12-item juice checklist), design §7, the kart-controller events (`driftTierUp`, `boostStart`, `hit`, `landed`, `wall`, `bump`), the race and item events, and the pmndrs postprocessing API._

**Pure layer (tested):**
- `trauma.ts` — trauma in [0, 1], added by events (hit 0.5, landing 0.2, boost 0.15, wall 0.25), decaying 1.6 per second; shake = trauma², offsets from a seeded smooth noise, **rotation a fraction of a degree** (max 0.6°), translation max 0.35 m.
- `camera.ts` — FOV kick on boost: +12° in 0.12 s, eased back over 0.6 s; −5° on a hit for 0.25 s; roll ±3° with drift direction.
- `director.ts` — events → effect cues: spark tier and colour, bursts (balloon pop, coin, hit stars, confetti), skid on/off per kart, hit-stop (75 ms, player only), finish slow-motion (0.3× for 1 s, player only).
- `timing.ts` — the sim-time scale the loop applies to the accumulator (hit-stop 0, slow-mo 0.3, else 1). The sim stays deterministic: only how many fixed ticks run per real second changes.

**Three layer (smoke-tested):**
- `particles.ts` — one `InstancedMesh` of camera-facing quads (billboard in the vertex shader), up to 2,048 live, per-instance colour and life, CPU integration with gravity and drag, additive blending. One draw call.
- `skids.ts` — a ring buffer of tyre-mark quads laid while drifting, multiplying the road darker and fading over 4 s (`SKID`). One draw call.
- `speedLines.ts` — thin streaks in camera space while boosting. One draw call.
- `post.ts` — one `EffectPass`: bloom (high threshold, so only sparks, flames and lamps glow), chromatic aberration (boost only), vignette, ACES tone mapping. Off on `quality: low`.

**Reduced motion** (ui-hud setting or OS): no shake, no speed lines, no chromatic aberration, no hit-stop, no slow-motion; FOV kick halved.

**Tests:** trauma decays to zero and never exceeds 1; shake rotation stays under 0.6°; FOV kick timing; director mapping per event; timing scale per state; reduced motion zeroes the right things; particle pool never exceeds capacity and recycles oldest first.

## Lessons (repair loop writes here)
_(error → cause → fix → rule; newest first)_
- 2026-09-25: **The flames vanished after one edit, with nothing in the log the capture read.** Cause: a GLSL syntax error (a new branch added after an `else` that still had no `if`): three logs a shader error once, to the console, and the mesh draws nothing. On the way, a GLSL comment inside a JS template literal quoted a name in backticks and ended the string (tsc caught that one). Fix: the branch; the capture helper now reads every program's `diagnostics.runnable` after each scenario. Rule: after any shader edit, check the programs' diagnostics in the page, not the picture alone; no backticks in GLSL held in a template literal.
- 2026-09-25: **The ignition's billows drew as a white cloud** (and the first nozzle flash as a white ball bigger than the kart's back). Cause: the flame's bands were 2.5 to 3 in linear light; the filmic tone map turns anything much past 1.5 toward white and the bloom (from 1.0) spreads it, so yellow and orange burned white; and the flash was two 0.6 m additive white discs held five frames. Fix: band colors with a top channel near 1.5 and weak channels near nothing (only the core and the nozzle's ring meant to glow), no extra gain on the billows, and a flash of a few frames drawn as jagged bolts round a small heart. Rule: judge an HDR color after the tone map and the bloom, in a still of the real frame, not by its hue in linear light (flames.test.ts holds the bands at 2 or under).
- 2026-09-25: **The podium's "3" was cut off by its step's top (review still).** Cause: every place plate was sized 0.82 of its block's whole height and centred on the whole block, but a block's front only shows between the 0.14 m band round its foot and the 0.12 m lip of its top; on the 0.5 m 3rd block that is 0.24 m, and the 0.41 m plate ran under both. Fix: `platePlace` fits and centres each plate in the front its block shows. Rule: place a decal by the face it sits on, trims taken off, never by the whole solid's height.
- 2026-09-24: **Firefox drew the grass's dark greens, Pip's teal kart and every balloon black (17 to 38% of the screen on Meadow, Canyon and Boardwalk); Chrome and Safari looked right.** Cause: the grade's HueSaturationEffect clamps only the top, so a strong colour's weakest channel went below 0, and the sRGB encode after the chain took pow() of it: NaN, which Chrome writes as 0 and Firefox as a black pixel. Found by toggling the chain's parts in headless Firefox (grade 0: clean). Fix: a FloorEffect last in the EffectPass, max(rgb, 0); Chrome's pixels identical on all six tracks (read back before and after). Rule: every post chain ends with no channel under 0, and a new effect is looked at in Firefox and WebKit too, not only Chrome.
- 2026-09-24: **The new streak sparks and embers drew nothing.** Cause: the streak shader mapped the quad's x onto the axis turned counter-clockwise, which mirrors it, so every spark was culled as a back face; the program linked and nothing logged. Found by drawing one spark alone into a render target and counting its pixels. Fix: across is the axis turned clockwise, (axis.y, -axis.x); particles.test.ts checks the winding for every axis. Rule: a quad laid out by its own shader keeps the plane's winding (or is DoubleSide), and is checked by its pixels.
- 2026-09-24: The boost speed lines never drew, and "no wind lines" was the critique. Cause: the vertex shader maps the quad's (x, y) onto (across, along) the streak, a mirror image, so every quad faced away from the camera and was back-face culled; no error, nothing on screen. Fix: `side: DoubleSide`. Rule: a mesh placed straight in clip space by its own shader is checked by drawing it alone into a render target and counting its pixels, not by the absence of errors.
