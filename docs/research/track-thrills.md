# Track design research: Mario Kart World and Mario Kart 8

## 1. Mario Kart World (MKW) track mechanics

- **Charge jump:** hold the drift button while going straight. Sparks go blue, then yellow, then rainbow, and a longer charge gives a longer small boost when you land. It is also how you get onto rails and walls. Steering while charging is limited on land but looser on water. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Charge_Jump), [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/mario-kart-world-charge-jump-jumping-mechanic-mkw/))
- **Rail grinding:** you get on by charge-jumping onto a rail, or by driving onto one that is embedded in the ground or joined to it at one end. Grinding builds a mini-turbo, but you only get the boost if you hop off with a direction pressed. Riding off the end gives nothing. ([GameRant](https://gamerant.com/mario-kart-world-grind-rail-ride-walls-wall-riding-mkw/)) Rails are everyday objects: guard rails, pipes, power lines. ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/mario-kart-world-rail-wall-charge-jump-ride-switch-2-1851784648))
- **Wall riding:** hit a wall at speed while in the air. The kart sticks and drives sideways "for a few seconds". It works on any surface and replaces MK8's anti-gravity. ([GameRant](https://gamerant.com/mario-kart-world-grind-rail-ride-walls-wall-riding-mkw/), [mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Anti-gravity)) Rideable walls include buildings, terrain and trucks on the road. ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/mario-kart-world-rail-wall-charge-jump-ride-switch-2-1851784648))
- **Tricks off anything:** a timed hop gives a trick and a boost off ramps, rails, bumps, sand dunes and waves. Wave tricks can be chained. ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/how-to-get-speed-boosts-in-mario-kart-world-all-methods/1100-6532327/))
- **Water:** wheels turn into water skis and the kart floats. Nintendo added whitecaps to wave crests so players can time their jumps. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Mario_Kart_World), [Ask the Developer ch.3](https://www.nintendo.com/au/news-and-articles/ask-the-developer-vol-18-mario-kart-world-chapter-3/))
- **Gliding:** the kart pops out wings. Glide ramps are cyan with scrolling white glider arrows. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Glide_Ramp))
- **Dash panels:** a pink-to-red gradient with white arrows. Some sit on the road and some on ramps. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Dash_Panel))
- **Rewind:** hold down on the D-pad to back up your own kart. Solo modes only, rivals keep driving, and it can't be used during a drift. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Rewind), [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/mario-kart-world-rewind-how-to/))
- **Time of day and weather:** a 24-minute day in Free Roam (14 minutes day, 2 sunset, 6 night, 2 sunrise), with rain and snow. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Mario_Kart_World), [Ask the Developer ch.2](https://www.nintendo.com/au/news-and-articles/ask-the-developer-vol-18-mario-kart-world-chapter-2/)) Whether weather changes grip is **unconfirmed**. The only claim comes from an article about a patent. ([4scarrs](https://www.4scarrsgaming.com/2026/09/nintendo-patent-regional-weather-mario-kart-world.html))
- **Moving surfaces:** conveyor belts in Toad's Factory, bouncy clouds in Great ? Block Ruins, DK bounce pads in DK Spaceport.
- **Destructible objects:** crates block a land shortcut on Peach Beach until someone smashes through. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Peach_Beach))
- **Traffic:** cars and trucks in Crown City, plus dash-panel ramp trucks you can use. Tractors in Toad's Factory take turns blocking the dash panels. Trains run on Whistlestop Summit and Rainbow Road. Boats circle a whirlpool on one route. ([Crown City](https://www.mariowiki.com/Crown_City), [Toad's Factory](https://www.mariowiki.com/Toad's_Factory), [Wario Shipyard](https://www.mariowiki.com/Wario_Shipyard))
- **Animals and enemies:** cows and moles in Moo Moo Meadows, elephants, zebras and giraffes on the Faraway Oasis routes, duck-like creatures (Cataquacks) and water-skiing Shy Guys on Peach Beach. ([Moo Moo](https://www.mariowiki.com/Moo_Moo_Meadows), [Faraway Oasis](https://www.mariowiki.com/Faraway_Oasis))
- **Knockout Tour:** 24 racers drive one long run through several courses. At each checkpoint the last four are knocked out. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Mario_Kart_World))
- **Falling off:** the rescue takes up to 3 coins. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Lakitu_(Mario_Kart_referee)))

## 2. MKW set pieces by course

- **Crown City:** traffic, ramp trucks on a bridge, rails across solar-panel rooftops, rails down the middle of stairs, a quarter pipe for a 180° turn, a glide ramp near the end. It has day and night versions. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Crown_City))
- **Whistlestop Summit:** a steam train circles the mountain and gets in the way. You can grind the train tracks instead of the road, chain wall rides from the left wall to the right, and charge-jump over a wall to skip a turn. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Whistlestop_Summit))
- **Peach Beach:** a plaza where you choose the ground or the rooftops, a grind rail that leads to a glide ramp, dash-panel ramps on the water, and the crate-blocked shortcut. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Peach_Beach))
- **Dino Dino Jungle:** a T. rex attacks, triceratops act as ramps, some dinosaurs work as rails, and erupting geysers can be tricked off if your timing is right. ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/mario-kart-world/dino-dino-jungle/), [Game8](https://game8.co/games/Mario-Kart-World/archives/527094))
- **DK Spaceport:** five levels joined by U-turns, a grid of rails with gaps you have to switch between, a giant robot throwing rolling barrels, and a glide all the way back down to the ground. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/DK_Spaceport))
- **Airship Fortress:** cannon shells that flip you and knock your item loose, wrench-throwing enemies popping out of hatches, moving columns of fire, and a cannon that fires you up a spiral tower with an open edge. ([Mario Kart fan wiki (Fandom), via search](https://mariokart.fandom.com/wiki/Airship_Fortress))
- **Boo Cinema:** you drive onto giant film strips inside the screen. The countdown on the screen runs down as you approach it and backs up if you drive away. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Boo_Cinema))
- **Rainbow Road:** a glide that reveals floating crystal islands, a shallow river that speeds you up, a rainbow half-pipe, a flying train that passes twice, and a space station with falling-block enemies. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Rainbow_Road_(Mario_Kart_World)))
- **Toad's Factory:** belt pairs running in opposite directions, crushers, and upper routes you can only reach by grinding. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Toad's_Factory))
- **Great ? Block Ruins and Starview Peak:** each splits into a high route and a low route. On Starview Peak the high road has the dash panels. A ramp sends you through a boost ring. ([Ruins](https://www.mariowiki.com/Great_%3F_Block_Ruins), [Starview](https://www.mariowiki.com/Starview_Peak))

## 3. Mario Kart 8 set pieces

- **Anti-gravity:** starts on marked panels. Tires turn sideways and glow blue. Bumping another kart or a spin pillar gives both a small boost and pushes them apart. The idea came from spinning tops knocking into each other. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Anti-gravity), [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2014/05/interview_mario_kart_8_director_kosuke_yabuki_on_key_features_of_the_wii_us_blockbuster_release)) Anti-gravity was often an optional branch up a wall (source: a search summary of the director interviews; **unconfirmed** which interview says it).
- **Glide ramps:** every new MK8 course has one except Toad Harbor, Excitebike Arena, Dragon Driftway and Mute City. Doing a trick on the ramp gives the boost at takeoff, not on landing. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Glide_Ramp))
- **Underwater:** a propeller pops out, the kart switches to its water handling stat, and entering water cancels a glide. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Underwater_driving))
- **Loops and inversions:** Mario Circuit is a Möbius strip, and a jumbo jet flies through Sunshine Airport. ([Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/konno-and-yabuki-on-how-f-zero-led-to-mario-kart-struggling-with-anti-gravity-in-mk8-sunshine-airport-came-from-previous-entrys-development/)) Corkscrews on MK8's Rainbow Road are **unconfirmed**.
- **Falling off:** the rescue catches you straight away instead of letting you fall off screen. Nintendo says you'll "lose a lot of time". The recovery boost from older games was removed. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Lakitu_(Mario_Kart_referee)), [Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.com/jp/ichikara/aabpa/index_en.html))
- **Placement:**
  - Mario Kart Stadium has 3 item rows, one of them on a ramp shortcut. It has dash panels on the outside line and 3 panels leading into the last ramp. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Mario_Kart_Stadium))
  - Toad Harbor has at least 2 dash panels per lap, 1 trick ramp and 2 shortcuts. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Toad_Harbor))
  - Double item boxes sit in hard-to-reach spots. ([Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.com/jp/ichikara/aabpa/index_en.html))
  - Many shortcuts are off-road cuts that only pay off with a boost item. ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Mario-Kart-World/archives/527094))

## 4. Design principles

- MKW's new moves exist to make driving "in a straight line" fun, and to "open up a multitude of driving possibilities for just a single wall". ([ch.2](https://www.nintendo.com/au/news-and-articles/ask-the-developer-vol-18-mario-kart-world-chapter-2/))
- Readability: the whitecaps were added because players couldn't tell where a wave's crest was. ([ch.3](https://www.nintendo.com/au/news-and-articles/ask-the-developer-vol-18-mario-kart-world-chapter-3/))
- Landmarks: rock formations mark a change of region, and a wide road narrows to reveal the next landmark. ([ch.2](https://www.nintendo.com/au/news-and-articles/ask-the-developer-vol-18-mario-kart-world-chapter-2/))
- The new techniques are optional. Players "still have fun playing with just the basic controls". There are 24 racers so you "see some competitive action here and there". ([Part 1](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/ask-the-developer-vol-18-mario-kart-world-part-1/))
- "Instantly enjoyable, but deep" was the guiding rule for Mario Kart 7. ([Iwata Asks MK7](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Iwata-Asks/Iwata-Asks-Mario-Kart-7/Vol-2-In-house-Staff/2-Instantly-Enjoyable-But-Deep/2-Instantly-Enjoyable-But-Deep-231022.html))
- Each course went through many iterations and "thousands of tests" for balance. ([Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2014/05/interview_mario_kart_8_director_kosuke_yabuki_on_key_features_of_the_wii_us_blockbuster_release))
- A breakdown of Bone-Dry Dunes found 4 route choices per lap and sections that get harder as the lap goes on. It singles out landing a jump straight into a corner as the main thing that frustrates players. ([case study](https://zoe-pilgrim.itch.io/kokiri-forest-mario-kart-8-level-design/devlog/339569/mario-kart-8-case-study))
- The main complaint about MKW: its connecting routes are "lengthy straightaway sections" that feel dull when you're in front. ([Wccftech](https://wccftech.com/review/mario-kart-world/))
- Small shortcuts on the final bends give players who are behind a chance to recover. Later games made roads wider and more forgiving than Super Mario Kart's tight hairpins. ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/-i-super-mario-kart-i-at-25-dissecting-a-revolutionary-game-design))

## 5. Numbers

- **MK8DX world records (150cc, 3 laps):** 1:33.6 to 2:01.4, so about 31–40 seconds per lap at world-record pace. ([mkwrs](https://mkwrs.com/mk8dx/)) About 40–50 seconds for casual players is **unconfirmed**.
- **MKW world records:** 1:28.6 (DK Spaceport) to 2:03.8 (Boo Cinema), and 3:52.8 for Rainbow Road. Whether these are 3-lap times is **unconfirmed**. ([mkwrs](https://mkwrs.com/mkworld/))
- **Dash panels:** at least 2 per lap on Toad Harbor. No count across all courses was found.
- **Item rows:** 3 on Mario Kart Stadium. That 2–3 is typical is **unconfirmed**.
- **Trick ramps:** 1 per lap on Toad Harbor. Glide ramps: 1 or more on nearly every new MK8 course.
- **Respawn time:** no official figure found (**unconfirmed**). In MK8 you are caught instantly. If you drive the wrong way for about 10–15 seconds, the game turns you around. ([mariowiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Lakitu_(Mario_Kart_referee)))

## Top 10 to copy in spirit (original versions)

Anything adopted still has to go into `docs/design.md` first, and should be checked against `docs/first-principles.md`.

1. **Grind rails with a hop-off boost:** harbor dock rails, boardwalk railings, sky-circuit girders. Paint every rail one bright color, and give the boost only when you hop off, not for riding to the end.
2. **Short wall-ride strips:** striped mesa cliffs and snowbank walls. Hop into one at speed, stick for about 1.5 seconds, pop off with a boost.
3. **Trick off anything with a timed hop:** waves with whitecaps on the harbor and boardwalk, dunes in the canyon, moguls on the snow pass. Allow chaining.
4. **Timed launch vents:** geysers in the canyon, steam vents on the pass. They glow before they erupt, and a trick at the top gives a boost.
5. **Traffic that doubles as terrain:** harbor delivery trucks with ramps on the back and sides you can ride.
6. **A scheduled crossing vehicle:** a freight train on the snow pass with a grindable roof, or a carnival tram. Warn with a sound cue and flashing lights.
7. **Glide gates over gaps:** cyan-style arrow panels over the mesa jump and between sky islands, with a boost if you trick at takeoff.
8. **High and low route splits about once per lap third:** harbor rooftops vs street, upper vs lower mesa. Put a boost-only cut on the final turn so trailing racers can catch up.
9. **A crate wall across a shortcut, plus roaming animals:** the first kart to smash the crates opens the cut for everyone. Meadow sheep cross on a clearly marked lane.
10. **Landmark reveals plus a quick respawn:** a road that narrows to reveal the Ferris wheel or a floating island, and an instant catch on falls (MK8 style) so the sky circuit and the mesa don't punish players too hard.
