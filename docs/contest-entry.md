## Skool post (paste as is)

**Rascal Rally!** 🏁 A cartoon kart racer you can play in your browser right now, built end to end with AI.

▶️ Play: https://adamwebsiteformula.github.io/kart-racer/
💻 Code: https://github.com/AdamWebsiteFormula/kart-racer

What's in it:
- 8 original racers, 6 tracks, and drifting with blue, orange and rainbow mini-turbos
- 13 original items, held two at a time: roll over rivals as a giant **Strike Ball**, boing over trouble on a **Pogo Spring**, hook the racer ahead with a **Grapple Anchor** and slingshot past
- A giant creature on every track: a lava-rock dinosaur whose stomp sends a shock ring across the road, a snowball-throwing yeti, a kraken that slams a tentacle across the boardwalk, and more
- Tracks built from research into what makes Mario Kart World's courses fun: a neon **loop-the-loop** you ride upside down, **geysers** that blast you into the air for a trick, rows of snow moguls and sand dunes to trick off, real ramps, glowing boost pads, and cliff edges with no walls (fall off and a giant claw carries you back)
- **Final Lap Shift:** on the last lap every track changes (a storm rolls in, the bridge goes down and the only way on is through a lantern-lit mine, the tide comes in)
- Grand Prix, Knockout (8 start, only 2 finish), Time Trial and a Daily Challenge with a global leaderboard
- Plays on keyboard, gamepad, and phone (turn it sideways)

How AI built it:
- **Claude Code** (Opus): designed and wrote the whole game: physics, AI rivals, items, tracks, UI, tests (569 of them) and deploy. It also ran teams of AI reviewers that hunted bugs and wrote the tests that prove the fixes
- **Higgsfield**: every racer, creature, landmark and item model (AI images turned into 3D), the painted skies and the item art
- **ElevenLabs**: all the music and every sound effect
- **Supabase**: the leaderboard

My favorite moment: hopping the dinosaur's shock ring on the last lap while a Strike Ball knocks the pack flying. Tell me your best time on the Daily Challenge!

## 60-second Loom script

Start the recording on the title screen (the camera tours the track by itself). Keep the game full screen.

| Time | Show on screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Title screen, camera sweeping over Harbor Loop | "This is Rascal Rally, a kart racer I built with AI, and you can play it in your browser right now." |
| 5–15 s | Quick Race on **Canyon Rush**: drift a corner until the sparks go rainbow, then boost | "Eight racers, six tracks, and drifting that feels right: hold the turn for blue, orange, then rainbow sparks." |
| 15–25 s | Keep racing: ride a geyser up for a trick, then hop the dinosaur's shock ring | "Geysers throw you in the air for a trick boost. And every track has a giant creature: this lava-rock dinosaur stomps, and you hop its shock wave." |
| 25–38 s | Use items: pop a balloon, show the roulette, fire a Beach Ball or a Wind-Up Mouse, then (from the back) a Strike Ball | "Thirteen original items, held two at a time, like the giant bowling ball that knocks everyone flying." |
| 38–44 s | Cut to **Boardwalk Nights**: the neon loop-the-loop (the camera swings out to watch you go upside down) | "There's a neon loop-the-loop at the carnival." |
| 44–48 s | Back on Canyon Rush, the last lap: the **Final Lap Shift** banner | "And on the last lap, every track changes. Here the bridge goes down." |
| 48–56 s | Results screen with the leaderboard, then flash the How to Play page | "Grand Prix, Knockout, Time Trial and a daily challenge with a global leaderboard." |
| 56–60 s | Back on the track | "Claude Code wrote it, Higgsfield made the 3D art, ElevenLabs made the music. Link's below. Beat my time!" |

Recording tips:
- Canyon Rush has the dinosaur and a clear Final Lap Shift. For a shorter take, pick Time Trial or use 150cc.
- To get a Strike Ball fast, stay near the back for the first lap (the back of the pack gets the big items).
- The Kraken on Boardwalk Nights (at night, with neon) is the best-looking creature shot if you have time for a second clip.
- For the loop shot, use Time Trial on Boardwalk Nights: the loop is on the straight right after the Kraken.
- To show the claw, drive off the left side of the Canyon Rush mesa top (about halfway round the lap).
