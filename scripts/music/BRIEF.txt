- Notation: "Bb4:8 C5:16. [F3 A3]:4^ r:8 E5:8~ E5:2 |" (C4 = middle C; lengths 1 2 4 8 16 32, dots, t triplet;
  flags > accent ^ marcato ' staccato !fall !doit !shake !scoop !bend !vib !sl; pp..ff; bar lines are checked).
  Use the template's lines([bar strings], t0) helper so ties cross bar lines and each bar is checked.
- LOOPS: INTRO bars play once, then LOOP bars repeat. The loop's last bar is the same music as the intro's last
  bar (a pickup written by one function called twice) and compose() calls s.twin(INTRO - 1, <loop's last bar>).
  The build then makes the wrap seamless (notes.json wrap.preMatch must be 0.0). The game replays a song from the
  top 1.06x faster and a semitone up on the final lap: keep leads out of the extreme top. 60-90 s in all.
- COURSE INTROS: each course candidate has shorts() returning {'intro-6s': (Song, 6.0, cadence_beat), 'intro-2s':
  (Song, 2.5, cadence_beat)}: a 6.0 s piece from the hook ending on a held chord that can fade (landing by ~4.3 s)
  and a 2.5 s flourish (a hit by ~1.0 s). The title and results slots do not need them.
- BUILD (renders, writes MP3/WAV/MIDI/notes.json, the intros, and screens everything into notes.json):
  python scripts/music/build.py <module> -> ~/.cache/rascal-music/candidates/<slot>/<candidate>/
- CHECKS: check_scores.py <module> (parses every bar, flags ranges); review.py <module> [--png] (harmonic clashes,
  piano roll to view with Read); stems.py scripts/music/songs/<file>.py (per-stem level and octave balance);
  tune.py <module> "{'tracks/bass/gain': -3}" ... (mix variants on cached stems scored for production quality;

  stays near 0, or the game skips the quiet opening.
